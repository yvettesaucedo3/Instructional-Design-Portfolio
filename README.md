# Instructional Design Portfolio
**Yvette Saucedo, M.Ed.** | Special Education & Curriculum | [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/yvettesaucedo)

Learning resources designed to support special education teams in running smooth, family-centered IEP/ARD meetings (ARD = Admission, Review and Dismissal, Texas's name for IEP meetings). The three pieces work together: the video introduces the topic, the micro-lesson teaches it, and the job aid is what teachers keep. All examples are fictional and contain no student information.

## 1. Microlearning: Preparing for a Successful ARD Meeting
**[Take the lesson](https://yvettesaucedo3.github.io/Instructional-Design-Portfolio/rise-lesson/)** (works on desktop and phone)

A short, mobile-friendly micro-lesson that walks teachers through what to do before, during, and after an ARD meeting. I designed each part to make teachers do something, not just read:

- **Process walkthrough:** four steps to prepare 2–3 weeks out
- **Tabs:** four habits for the meeting itself
- **Flip cards:** "say this, not that" examples that turn vague comments into clear, data-based ones
- **Drag-and-drop sort:** accommodation or modification?
- **Accordion:** follow-through steps after the meeting, including how to share the plan securely
- **Knowledge check:** three questions with feedback
- **Download:** the job aid below

**Tools:** Articulate Rise 360 (also exported as a SCORM 1.2 package for LMS use)  
**Audience:** General and special education teachers

## 3. Job Aid: How to Prepare for an ARD Meeting
A one-page checklist that walks teachers through what to do before, during, and after an ARD meeting, with key terms in plain language and "say this, not that" examples for talking with families.

**Tool:** Canva  
**Audience:** General and special education teachers
