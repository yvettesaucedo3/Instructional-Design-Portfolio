## Math Intervention & MTSS

### Math Intervention Decision Guides (Leader + Teacher versions)
Two companion guides built on the National Center on Intensive Intervention's Data-Based Individualization (DBI) process. The leader version helps coaches and MTSS leads decide what to do when a student isn't responding to Tier 2 math intervention: check fidelity, dosage, access and data quality, coach the teacher, and know when to refer. The teacher version turns the same process into what to do in each session: teach with the CRA sequence, track progress weekly, and know when to bring a student to the MTSS team.

**Tool:** Canva  
**Audience:** Instructional coaches, MTSS leads, campus leaders, and intervention teachers

## ARD Meeting Preparation
Learning resources designed to support special education teams in running smooth, family-centered IEP/ARD meetings (ARD = Admission, Review and Dismissal, Texas's name for IEP meetings). The three pieces work together: the video introduces the topic, the micro-lesson teaches it, and the job aid is what teachers keep.

## 1. Microlearning: Preparing for a Successful ARD Meeting
**[Take the lesson](https://yvettesaucedo3.github.io/Instructional-Design-Portfolio/rise-lesson/)** (works on desktop and phone)

A short, mobile-friendly micro-lesson that walks teachers through what to do before, during, and after an ARD meeting. I designed each part to make teachers do something, not just read:

- **Process walkthrough:** four steps to prepare 2–3 weeks out
- **Tabs:** four habits for the meeting itself
- **Flip cards:** "say this, not that" examples that turn vague comments into clear, data-based ones
- **Drag-and-drop sort:** accommodation or modification?
- **Accordion:** follow-through steps after the meeting, including how to share the plan securely
- **Knowledge check:** three questions with feedback
- **Download:** the job aid below

**Tools:** Articulate Rise 360 (also exported as a SCORM 1.2 package for LMS use)  
**Audience:** General and special education teachers

## Implementation & Data

### Implementation Case Study: Embrace IEP + Journify Rollout
How I took two compliance platforms at a new school from vendor demos to go-live and lasting adoption: a manual SIS import across four modules and then an automated SFTP feed, role permissions for legal records, Loom videos so busy providers could onboard on their own, and a 15-step ARD tracker. 16 ARD meetings completed in the first 8 weeks, 99.6% of testing accommodations delivered, and teacher feedback that led Journify to add bulk entry for all users.

[View the case study (PDF)](Implementation_Case_Study_Embrace_Journify.pdf)

### ARD Case Study: Annual ARD + REED with a Placement Change
A complete IEP I wrote for a fictional 7th grader with a learning disability, dyslexia, and dysgraphia. It shows how I turn evaluation data into clear present levels, write measurable TEKS-aligned goals, justify a placement change with data, and write a Prior Written Notice and deliberations that families can understand. All student, family, and school information is fictional.
